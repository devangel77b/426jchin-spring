\begin{abstract}
Girl in Space Club is a creative technology and human-systems design lab. They design, prototype, and commercialize equipment, tools, and experiences for people navigating future worlds—on Earth and beyond. It was founded by NASA engineer Sabrina Thompson. As part of our mission, we were tasked with creating a simulation tool that models spacecraft swarms powered by orbital mechanics, presented to users using basketball terminology and representations to aid in understanding of how swarms function. This will be used to demonstrate satellite swarm mechanics as a learning tool for users to learn about swarms  During our presentation, we will present (1) the basic structure of the backend of our swarm engine; (2) the process behind building the user interface as well as the actual interface itself; and (3) demonstrations of how our final product works. 
\end{abstract}
